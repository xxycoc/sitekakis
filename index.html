Kopi with your kakis

A simple “Hi, mind if I join you?” is a good start.

  • Ask first. Offer company or a drink without expecting a yes.
  • Keep it short. Respect breaks, privacy and anyone who wants quiet time.
  • Keep it simple. Ask what they like: kopi, teh or 100 Plus. Sharing a meal is welcome if they are keen.
  • Make a friend. Listen, chat, and ask before taking any photos.
